Yan Yang – Independent Researcher & Experienced SDE

I am an independent researcher with graduate-level training in computer science, previous experience as a Machine Learning R&D Engineer at Deepcell Inc. (a Stanford spun-out), and previous research assistant experience at Georgetown University, KAUST (King Abdullah University of Science and Technology), and the University of Nevada, Reno. I am equally open to doctoral research positions and industry roles that support research work.

My research spans two main threads. The first is computational modeling of motivation and emotion, approached through tools from machine learning and statistical modeling grounded in psychology. Within this thread, I'm interested in extending statistical models into richly individualized, profile-style characterizations. I'm also drawn to studying user behavior on games and other rich-sensor platforms. The second is PDE inverse problems and Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs), including a purely theoretical project on disentangling the functional components of PINNs (with experimental validation planned).

As an independent researcher, I have:

• Written single-authored articles across these research threads.
• Had peer-reviewed papers accepted by a psychology journal and by the AI4Physics Workshop at ICML 2026 (the latter with code released on GitHub).
• A manuscript currently under review at TMLR (Transactions on Machine Learning Research), alongside multiple non-refereed research notes on Zenodo.

My earlier work includes a published paper on facial recognition (computer vision), as well as research on user behavior — including detecting searcher struggle from interaction signals — contributing to quantitative UX/user-behavior modeling. I am open to collaborative opportunities and academic mentorship, and actively seeking doctoral or industry research positions in Canada, Europe, England, New Zealand, Australia, the United States, and other regions.
